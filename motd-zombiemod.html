🧟 Zombie Mod 🧟

Day of Defeat: Source - v0.7.156 BETA

📋 How It Works

At the start of each round, one player becomes a zombie. Everyone else is human.

When a zombie kills a human, that human becomes a zombie.

The round ends when all humans are dead, or time runs out.

🎮 Basic Rules

  • One zombie at round start
  • Everyone else is human
  • Zombies can break down most objects, walls and doors
  • Humans have guns, zombies have spade
  • Humans can move props by right clicking on them with their knife. Press and hold to pick up, release to drop
  • Survive as long as possible!

⚔️ Combat & Features

🎯 Critical Headshots

Pistol headshots deal critical damage to zombies!

When you land a critical hit:

  • Zombie reduced to critical health (2 HP)
  • Finish them off for bonus rewards!

🎁 Kill Rewards

Killing zombies earns you rewards:

  • +15 Health (up to max 150 HP)
  • +1 Pistol Clip (Colt/P38, max 3 clips)
  • +0.02 Speed Boost (stacks, max +0.25 total)

Finish Critical Zombies: Double rewards!

🔍 Human Skills NEW

Humans can select a skill from the equipment menu:

  • ESP Skill: See zombie class names when aiming at them
  • Reveals Gas and TNT zombies before they die
  • Ghost zombies remain hidden (stealth advantage)
  • Skill resets each spawn

Without ESP: All zombies show generic name/health

With ESP: "Gas Zombie: PlayerName (HP)" or "TNT Zombie: PlayerName (HP)"

📦 Supply Drops (Pickups)

Pickups spawn randomly with a sky beam effect:

🟢 Green Beam = Health Pickup

Restores +50 HP (up to max 150 HP)

🟡 Yellow Beam = Ammo Pickup

Gives +1 clip for all weapons (up to max ammo)

🔵 Blue Beam = Speed Pickup

Grants +0.10 speed boost (lasts until death, max +0.25 total)

🔴 Red Beam = Damage Pickup

Increases damage by 50% (lasts 30 seconds)

Pickups auto-destroy after 30 seconds if not collected, or if a Zombie walks over them first

🧟 Special Zombie Classes

Bot Zombies have a 50% chance to spawn as a special class with unique abilities!

Players: Choose your class from the menu when you spawn as zombie

Bots: Get random classes automatically

GAS ZOMBIE UPGRADED

Active Ability: Right-click to spawn gas cloud at aim position!

  • Cooldown: 15 seconds between uses
  • Range: Unlimited (wherever you aim)
  • Tactical: Block doorways, cover retreat, area denial

Death Ability: Creates toxic gas cloud on death

  • Radius: 150 units
  • Duration: 10 seconds
  • Damage: 5 HP per second

Combo Potential: Use ability + die = 2 gas clouds!

TNT ZOMBIE

Explodes on death, damaging nearby players and breaking props!

  • Radius: 300 units
  • Max Damage: 150 HP (at center)
  • Friendly fire: YES (damages other zombies too!)
  • Strategy: Rush into groups of humans

GHOST ZOMBIE

Nearly invisible with oscillating transparency!

  • Alpha: 0-50 (extremely hard to see)
  • No name/health display for humans (even with ESP)
  • Perfect stealth: Ambush and flank
  • Tip: Stay still in dark areas for maximum effect

🌀 Zombie Teleportation

All zombies have a 25% chance to teleport to human spawn on respawn!

  • Activates 60 seconds after round start
  • Spark effect and sound when teleporting
  • Prevents camping in spawn areas
  • Adds unpredictability to zombie spawns

💡 Strategy Tips

For Humans:

  • Select ESP skill - identify Gas/TNT zombies before they kill you
  • Watch for gas clouds - Gas zombies can deploy them with right-click!
  • Keep distance from TNT zombies - they explode on death
  • Ghost zombies are nearly invisible - listen for footsteps and movement
  • Aim for headshots - pistol crits reduce zombies to 2 HP
  • Grab pickups - follow the colored beams for power-ups
  • Stick together - but spread out vs TNT zombies
  • Manage your ammo - pistols limited to 3 clips (21 rounds)

For Zombies:

  • Choose your class wisely - each has different playstyles
  • Gas Zombie: Pre-place clouds before engaging, block escape routes
  • TNT Zombie: Rush into groups, coordinate with teammates for max damage
  • Ghost Zombie: Ambush from dark corners, stay still to be invisible
  • Use teleportation - 25% chance to spawn behind enemy lines
  • Coordinate attacks - overwhelm isolated humans

🎮 Equipment & Skill Menu

At spawn, customize your loadout:

  • Primary Weapon: Rifle, SMG, MG, Sniper, etc.
  • Pistol: Colt (7 rounds/clip) or P38 (8 rounds/clip)
  • Equipment Bonus: Extra pistol ammo (adds 1 clip)
  • Human Skill: ESP (see zombie classes) or No Skill

Choose wisely - your loadout and skill affect your survival!

📊 Zombie Info Display

Aim at a zombie to see their info:

  • Without ESP: "PlayerName (HP)"
  • With ESP: "Gas Zombie: PlayerName (HP)" or "TNT Zombie: PlayerName (HP)"
  • Ghost Zombies: No display (hidden from all humans)
  • Critical Status: Shows when zombie has 2 HP or less
