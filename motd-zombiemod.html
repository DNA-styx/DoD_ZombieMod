🧟 Zombie Mod 🧟

Day of Defeat: Source - v0.9.18 BETA

📋 How It Works

At the start of each round, one player becomes a zombie. Everyone else is human.

When a zombie kills a human, that human becomes a zombie.

Humans have guns, zombies have melee only

The round ends when all humans are dead, or time runs out.

Zombies can break down most objects and doors

⚔️ Combat & Features

🎯 Critical Headshots

Pistol headshots deal critical damage to zombies!

Finish them off for bonus rewards!

🎁 Kill Rewards

Killing zombies earns you rewards:

  • +15 Health (up to max 150 HP)
  • +1 Pistol Clip (Colt/P38, max 3 clips)
  • +0.02 Speed Boost (stacks, max +0.25 total)

Finish Critical Zombies: Double rewards!

🔍 Human Skills & Abilities

Barricade Builder — available to all humans automatically:

  • Right-click with knife while aiming at a prop to pick it up
  • Right-click again to release — build barricades, block doorways, slow zombies

ESP Skill — selectable from the equipment menu:

  • See zombie class names when aiming at them
  • Right-click with pistol (Colt) to broadcast zombie class info to team
  • Ghost zombies remain hidden even with ESP

📦 Supply Drops (Pickups)

Pickups spawn randomly with a sky beam:

🟢 Green Beam = Health Pickup

Restores Health

🟡 Yellow Beam = Ammo Pickup

Gives extra ammo for all weapons

🔵 Blue Beam = Speed Pickup

Grants speed boost

🔴 Red Beam = Damage Pickup

Increases damage applied to Zombies

Pickups auto-destroy after 30 seconds if not collected or Zombie gets to them first

🧟 Special Zombie Classes

Zombies have a 50% chance to spawn as a special class with unique abilities!

Players: Choose your class from the menu when you spawn as zombie

Bots: Get random classes automatically

GAS ZOMBIE

Active Ability: Right-click to spawn gas cloud at aim position!

  • Cooldown: 15 seconds between uses
  • Tactical: Block doorways, cover retreat, area denial

Death Ability: Creates toxic gas cloud on death

TNT ZOMBIE

Explodes on death, damaging nearby players and breaking props!

GHOST ZOMBIE

Nearly invisible!

No name/health display for humans (even with ESP)

🌀 Zombie Teleportation

All zombies have a chance to teleport to human spawn on respawn!

💡 Strategy Tips

For Humans:

  • Build barricades - right-click with knife to move props, block doorways and slow zombies
  • Select ESP skill - identify Gas/TNT zombies before they kill you
  • Watch for gas clouds - Gas zombies can deploy them with right-click!
  • Keep distance from TNT zombies - they explode on death
  • Ghost zombies are nearly invisible - listen for footsteps and movement
  • Aim for headshots - pistol crits zombies
  • Grab pickups - follow the colored beams for power-ups
  • Stick together - but spread out vs TNT zombies
  • Manage your ammo - pistols is limited

For Zombies:

  • Choose your class wisely - each has different playstyles
  • Gas Zombie: Pre-place clouds before engaging, block escape routes
  • TNT Zombie: Rush into groups, coordinate with teammates for max damage
  • Ghost Zombie: Ambush from dark corners, stay still to be invisible
  • Use teleportation - random chance to spawn behind enemy lines
  • Coordinate attacks - overwhelm isolated humans

🎮 Humans Equipment & Skill Menu

At spawn, customize your loadout:

  • Primary Weapon: Rifle, SMG, MG, Sniper, etc.
  • Pistol: Colt or P38
  • Equipment Bonus: Extra pistol ammo
  • Human Skill: ESP and Third Party Plugin Skills

📊 Zombie Info Display

Aim at a zombie to see their info:

  • Without ESP: just the Zombie Name and Health
  • With ESP: as well as the Zombie Name and Heath you will see what Class they are!
  • Ghost Zombies: No display (hidden from all humans)
