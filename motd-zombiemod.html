How It Works

At the start of each round, one player becomes a zombie. Everyone else is human.

Zombies infect humans on kill. The round ends when all humans are dead or time runs out.

Humans are armed. Zombies fight melee-only but can break most objects and doors.

Kill Rewards

Each zombie kill rewards you. Finish a critical zombie for double.

+15 Health (max 150) +1 Pistol Clip (max 3) +0.02 Speed (max +0.25)

Pistol headshots deal critical damage — finish them off for double rewards.

Zombie Classes
Gas Zombie

Right-click to deploy a gas cloud. Cooldown 15s. Creates a cloud on death.

TNT Zombie

Explodes on death, damaging nearby players and breaking props.

Ghost Zombie

Nearly invisible. No name or health display — hidden even from ESP.

50% chance to spawn as a special class. Players choose from a menu; bots are assigned randomly.

All zombies have a chance to teleport to human spawn on respawn.

Pickups spawn randomly with a sky beam. Auto-destroy after 30 seconds or if a zombie reaches them first.

Skills & Equipment

Barricade Builder (all humans) — right-click with knife to pick up and move props. Block doorways, slow zombies.

ESP Skill (selectable) — see zombie class when aiming at them. Right-click with pistol to broadcast class info to your team. Ghost zombies are hidden even with ESP.

At spawn, choose your primary weapon, pistol (Colt or P38), equipment bonus, and skill.

Strategy

Humans

  • Build barricades to slow zombie advances
  • Use ESP to identify Gas and TNT zombies
  • Stay clear of dying TNT zombies
  • Listen for Ghost zombie movement
  • Pistol headshots deal critical damage
  • Follow beam colours to find pickups

Zombies

  • Gas: Pre-place clouds, block escape routes
  • TNT: Rush groups for splash damage
  • Ghost: Ambush from dark corners
  • Coordinate attacks on isolated humans
  • Use teleport chance to flank spawn
