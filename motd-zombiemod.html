🧟 Zombie Mod 🧟

Day of Defeat: Source - v0.7.107 BETA

📋 How It Works

At the start of each round, one player becomes a zombie. Everyone else is human.

When a zombie kills a human, that human becomes a zombie.

The round ends when all humans are dead, or time runs out.

🎮 Basic Rules

  • One zombie at round start
  • Everyone else is human
  • Zombies can break down some walls and doors
  • Humans have guns, zombies have melee (spade)
  • Survive as long as possible!

⚔️ Combat & Features

🛡️ Zombie Spawn Protection

Zombies are green and translucent when protected (default: 5 seconds after spawning).

You cannot damage them during this time. Wait for the green effect to disappear!

Note: Protection is removed if the zombie attacks.

🎯 Critical Headshots

Pistol headshots (Colt & P38) deal critical damage to zombies!

When you land a critical hit:

  • Zombie's helmet pops off
  • Zombie reduced to critical health (2 HP)
  • Finish them off for bonus rewards!

Also works with: Knives, Rifles, Rocket Launchers

🎁 Kill Rewards

Killing zombies earns you rewards:

  • +15 Health (up to max 150 HP)
  • +1 Pistol Clip (Colt/P38, max 3 clips)
  • +0.02 Speed Boost (stacks, max +0.25 total)

Finish Critical Zombies: Double rewards!

📦 Supply Drops (Pickups)

Pickups spawn randomly with a dramatic sky beam effect:

🟢 Green Beam = Health Pickup

Restores +50 HP (up to max 150 HP)

🟡 Yellow Beam = Ammo Pickup

Gives +1 clip for all weapons (up to max ammo)

🔵 Blue Beam = Speed Pickup

Grants +0.10 speed boost (lasts until death, max +0.25 total)

🔴 Red Beam = Damage Pickup

Increases damage by 50% (lasts 30 seconds)

Pickups auto-destroy after 30 seconds if not collected

🧟 Special Zombie Classes

Zombies have a 50% chance to spawn as a special class:

GAS ZOMBIE

Creates a toxic gas cloud on death that damages humans over time.

  • Radius: 150 units
  • Duration: 10 seconds
  • Damage: 5 HP per second

TNT ZOMBIE

Explodes on death, damaging nearby players and breaking props!

  • Radius: 300 units
  • Max Damage: 150 HP (at center)
  • Friendly fire: YES (damages other zombies too!)

📊 Zombie Info Display

Aim at a zombie to see:

  • Player name
  • Current health
  • Special class type (Gas/TNT)
  • Critical status (if applicable)

💡 Strategy Tips

  • Stick together - strength in numbers, but watch out for TNT zombies!
  • Watch for green zombies - they're spawn protected, save your ammo
  • Aim for headshots - pistol crits reduce zombies to 2 HP
  • Grab pickups - follow the colored beams for power-ups
  • Beware special classes - Gas clouds linger, TNT zombies explode!
  • Use the environment - some areas are easier to defend
  • Listen for audio cues - spawns, deaths, and pickups all have sounds
  • Manage your ammo - pistols limited to 3 clips (21 rounds)

🎮 Equipment Menu

At spawn, choose your loadout:

  • Primary Weapon: Rifle, SMG, MG, Sniper, etc.
  • Pistol: Colt (7 rounds/clip) or P38 (8 rounds/clip)
  • Equipment Bonus: Extra pistol ammo (adds 1 clip)

Choose wisely - your loadout affects your survival!
